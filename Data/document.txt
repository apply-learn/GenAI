ACME Corporation Employee Leave Policy

Overview:
ACME Corporation provides employees with a generous and fair leave policy to support work-life balance and wellbeing.

Annual Leave:
- Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Part-time employees receive prorated annual leave based on hours worked.
- Annual leave may be carried over up to 5 days into the following year with manager approval.

Sick Leave:
- Employees are allocated 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
- For extended illness, employees should contact HR to discuss long-term options and possible documentation.

Vacation Approval Process:
- Employees must submit vacation requests through the company portal at least 2 weeks in advance for single-day requests and 4 weeks for requests longer than 5 consecutive days.
- Managers review requests considering team coverage and project timelines.
- Managers should approve or decline requests within 5 business days. If a manager does not respond, the request should be escalated to HR.

Unpaid Leave and Special Circumstances:
- Employees may request unpaid leave for personal reasons; such requests are handled on a case-by-case basis.
- Maternity, paternity, and medical leaves follow statutory guidelines and company-sponsored benefits.

Contact:
For questions about leave balances or the approval process, contact HR at hr@acmecorp.example.
